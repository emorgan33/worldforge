# Collection brief: painterly fantasy

Status: proposed sourcing direction. No candidate is approved.

## Intent

Give a GM a small, coherent set of atmospheric portraits and places that can sit
beside personal artwork without competing with it. Stock art is optional and
must never be applied automatically.

## Family criteria

| Attribute | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Restrained earth, stone, moss, and jewel accents; avoid neon saturation unless an entry is deliberately isolated. |
| Lighting | Soft directional light with readable values; avoid crushed blacks that disappear against Night watch surfaces. |
| Edges | Visible painterly edges that remain legible in a cover crop; avoid busy edge-to-edge detail. |
| Texture | Brushwork and canvas grain are welcome; avoid photobashed or plastic surfaces. |
| Framing | A clear focal subject that survives centered narrow and wide crops. |
| Detail | Medium detail at full size; category candidates need a strong silhouette at 16px. |

## Coverage targets

- Portraits: varied ages, skin tones, gender presentations, and roles. Avoid a
  collection of only youthful warriors. Appearance must not encode villainy,
  competence, or status.
- Locations/landscapes: at least one subject that works in body content, one
  that survives a wide banner crop, and one that can be read as a small category
  illustration.
- Objects: iconic silhouettes for items, artifacts, and props.
- Category artwork: uncluttered, high-contrast silhouettes with a quiet
  background; reject detail-heavy candidates that become indistinct at 16px.

## Admission rules

- Rights-cleared CC0/public-domain candidates with reviewed evidence only.
- No official game artwork, recognizable protected characters, fan art, or
  generated imagery.
- Do not recolor or otherwise derive artwork for coherence unless rights allow
  it and the derivative is recorded in the manifest.
- Prefer dropping a candidate over admitting a questionable image to meet a
  count.
- A failed banner crop does not necessarily exclude an image from body use; a
  candidate can be recommended for some placements and not others.
