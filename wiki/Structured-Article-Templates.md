# Structured Article Templates

Choose a template when creating an article. Character, Creature, Spell, Item,
and Encounter provide optional rules-aware fields for D&D 5e 2014 and Pathfinder
2e Remaster. Species remains useful for ecology/culture rather than a creature
statblock. Characters use the [interactive sheet](Character-Sheets).

- **Creatures:** D&D CR/ability scores differ from Pathfinder level/attribute
  modifiers and Fortitude/Reflex/Will.
- **Spells:** D&D level/components/concentration differ from Pathfinder
  rank/traits/traditions/heightening.
- **Items:** D&D attunement/charges differ from Pathfinder level/Bulk/activation.
- **Encounters:** record party, difficulty/threat, XP, objectives, opposition,
  terrain, and outcome. XP fields are notes, not encounter calculators.

Enter only what you need. Blank optional values are allowed; changed invalid
numeric values pause autosave. Correct them to resume saving.

Changing template/profile preserves prose and metadata; inactive/custom fields
remain in **Other saved details** for authors. **Insert template outline** adds
starter headings only to an empty article, never over existing prose/images/tables.

Characters, creatures, spells, and items may link matching compendium entries
from the same world/system/category. The preview uses the current entry;
missing/mismatched links show a notice. These links do not calculate sheet
statistics or merge compendium mechanics into your notes.

Reader projections omit unknown and inactive fields. Use GM-note blocks for
private prose, not a custom field you assume is secret forever.
