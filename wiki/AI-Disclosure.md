# AI Disclosure

AI tools contributed to WorldForge's code and documentation. AI-assisted work
can contain errors or omissions even when automated checks pass. Maintainers
decide what ships; AI assistance is not a guarantee of correctness, security,
or fitness for a particular campaign.

Review software for your use, keep backups, and report reproducible problems.
Never send private campaign content, setup links, credentials, or personal data
to an AI service merely to obtain troubleshooting help. Use minimal synthetic
examples instead. Security issues follow the repository's private reporting
process, not public tickets.

The repository's [AI disclosure](https://github.com/emorgan33/worldforge/blob/main/docs/ai-disclosure.md)
is authoritative for the project disclosure. It is not a claim that bundled
third-party art is AI-generated or rights-cleared; see
[Images and Stock Art](Images-and-Stock-Art) for separate provenance/review rules.
